# 0.3.6 — Choose the section before searching

- **Find closest by** now sits above **Find similar articles**, so you choose what to compare before the search. Choose a section to search by that section alone, or keep **All sections (average)**.
- A manuscript lists only its identified sections, and the list follows any change you make to their roles. An indexed article lists all sections, and explains when the article does not have the one you chose.
- The results page offers **Search again by**, and its title names the section used. A section choice searches the full eligible collection; it does not reorder an earlier list.
- The previous "overall similarity" is now labelled **All sections (average)**. It is the same score, the mean over the sections both articles have, and is not a separate full-text comparison.
- The bundled index, retrieval and scoring are unchanged.
