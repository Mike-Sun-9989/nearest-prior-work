    How to read these results
    Rank
    List positions reflect the current search and filters. Section ranks in the details refer to all indexed articles with that section, excluding the source article for DOI searches.
    Section coverage
    Overall similarity averages the scores of the sections both papers have. Missing sections are not scored as zero. A high result based on one shared section has narrower coverage.
    Detailed scores
    z measures distance above the mean of random article pairs, in standard deviations. Percentile is the share of random pairs that are less similar; it is not a percentage of matching content. For short passages, use rank rather than these full-section reference scores.
    Interpretation
    Similarity is a starting point for reading, not a judgment of novelty. The index supplies metadata and scores, not article abstracts or full text.
    The article index and similarity search run on your computer. Manuscript text is sent to your chosen model only when you identify sections. Files are removed after local extraction; extracted text stays temporarily in memory. Opening an article link uses the publisher’s website.
