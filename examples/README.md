# Synthetic manuscript

`demo_manuscript.md` and `demo_manuscript.docx` contain an original synthetic example. The sample size, data and findings are invented to demonstrate section identification and retrieval; they are not an empirical result or a published paper.

Choose the Word file in the app and follow [the quickstart](../docs/quickstart.md). The app identifies structure with your selected language model, so this step uses your subscription/API allowance. The source text and heading styles can be regenerated with `python examples/make_demo_docx.py` after installing the app's dependencies.
